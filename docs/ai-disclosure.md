### What AI was used for

AI assistance was used to write and review application code, tests and
documentation under direct human direction. All model *training* runs on
synthetic data generated by this repository's own scripts — no AI service is
in the inference path for the fraud decision itself.

### What AI is **not** used for

- The fraud classifier, anomaly detector and risk modules are ordinary trained
  models and deterministic rules, not LLM calls.
- The LLM (optional Gemini) may only produce narrative explanation text. It
  cannot score, decide, or act. See `docs/architecture.md`.
